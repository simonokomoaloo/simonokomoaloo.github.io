View Research Download CV Get in Touch → Simon Okomo Aloo 28 Publications 790 Citations h15 H-Index 24 Q1 Articles // 01 — Profile About Me The researcher behind the science Simon Aloo earned a Bachelor of Science with First Class Honours in Food Science and Nutrition from Karatina University (Kenya), and both a master’s degree and PhD in Food Science and Biotechnology from Kangwon National University (Korea). His PhD research focused on the development of industrial hempseed as a functional food ingredient. He investigated the effects of dehulling, defatting, germination, and fermentation on the chemical composition and bioactive properties of hempseed. He was among the first researchers to report on the health benefits of fermented hempseed, demonstrating its potential as a functional food for the prevention and management of metabolic and neurological disorders. Driven by a strong passion for translational and medical research, Simon is currently pursuing a second doctoral program in biomedicine at Monash University in collaboration with the Baker Heart and Diabetes Institute. His current research focuses on lipid metabolism, stable isotope tracing, and the genetic regulation of cardiometabolic diseases. Simon has received several outstanding academic achievement awards and multiple international scholarships in recognition of his academic excellence and research contributions. Beyond research, he is the founder of ACMHR-K (Alliance for Cardiometabolic Health and Research–Kenya), a national non-profit organization dedicated to advancing research, education, mentorship, and advocacy on the prevention and treatment of cardiometabolic diseases.
